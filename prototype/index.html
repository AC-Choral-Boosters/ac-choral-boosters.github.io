About Us

The American Canyon Choral Boosters supports students in the ACHS choir program. Our funds help cover music, festival and competition fees, travel, costumes, accompanists, and scholarships so every student can participate.

Raising money for music, uniforms, travel, and student scholarships.

Volunteering

Helping at concerts, festivals, and events throughout the year.

Community

Connecting families, students, and supporters of choral music.
